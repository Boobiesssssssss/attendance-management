# User Manual

## Sign in
1. Open the application URL in a supported browser.
2. Enter your assigned username and password.
3. Do not share your account or password.

## Administrator
- **Dashboard:** overview of students, classes, teachers, and recent attendance.
- **Users:** create teacher/admin accounts and deactivate/reactivate accounts. Use unique credentials and share them privately.
- **Classes:** create a class/section and assign teachers to classes.
- **Subjects:** add subject names.
- **Students:** add and update admission number, full name, class, and section. Archive students who should no longer appear in new registers; their history is retained.
- **Attendance:** select class/date, review each status, and save.
- **Reports:** select a date range and optional class/student filter; export results as CSV.

## Teacher
- Sign in with the assigned teacher account.
- Dashboard shows assigned classes only.
- Select **Mark attendance**, choose class and date, set Present / Absent / Leave for each student, and save.
- Open **Reports** to view attendance for assigned classes.
- Contact the administrator if the correct class is not available.

## Attendance percentage
This prototype calculates Present ÷ Marked days × 100. Leave is included among marked days but is not counted as present. Confirm school policy before using reports for official decisions.

## Troubleshooting
- **Login fails:** check username/password with the administrator.
- **No classes listed:** ask admin to assign classes to the teacher.
- **No student appears:** check class selection, student active status, and search terms.
- **Website unavailable:** contact the project administrator; hosting free tiers may sleep or have quotas.
- **Reports differ from expectations:** check date range and the school's agreed calculation rule.

## Data privacy
Use only authorized accounts. Avoid downloading or sharing student reports unnecessarily. This academic prototype should not hold real student data until the school approves it and security, backup, privacy, and recovery controls are reviewed.
